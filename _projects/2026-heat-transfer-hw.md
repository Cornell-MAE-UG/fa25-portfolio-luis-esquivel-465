## Overview
In this assignment, I analyzed steady-state 1-D heat transfer through a two-wall composite system. Wall A had uniform volumetric heat generation and an insulated left surface, while Wall B transferred heat to a surrounding coolant by convection. I solved for the outer surface temperature, the volumetric heat generation rate, and the thermal conductivity of Wall A, then sketched the temperature distribution across both walls.

## What I Did
- Applied steady-state energy balances
- Used Fourier’s Law for conduction through Wall B
- Used a convection boundary condition at the outer surface
- Solved the heat diffusion equation with generation for Wall A
- Applied boundary conditions to determine constants and material properties
- Plotted and interpreted the temperature profile through the composite wall

## What I Learned
This problem helped me better understand how conduction, internal heat generation, and convection work together in one thermal system. I learned why temperature varies parabolically in a wall with heat generation and linearly in a wall without generation. I also strengthened my understanding of how insulation, interface continuity, and convection boundary conditions shape the temperature distribution and allow unknown thermal properties to be determined.

## Key Results
- Surface temperature of Wall B: **50°C**
- Volumetric heat generation: **50,000 W/m³**
- Thermal conductivity of Wall A: **150 W/m·K**
